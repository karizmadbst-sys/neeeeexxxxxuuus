**WEAPON SYSTEM, RELOADING, INVENTORY AND ZONE FIXES**

### 1. SCOPE SYSTEM
Only the following weapons should have a scope/zoom aiming feature:
- SCAR
- AK
- PARAFAL
- AWM

All other weapons must use normal aiming without a scope or zoom.

Make sure the scope button only works when one of these four weapons is equipped.

### 2. REALISTIC AND FASTER RELOADING
Improve the weapon reloading system:
- Make reloading slightly faster than the current system.
- Add realistic weapon-specific reload animations.
- Make the character correctly remove the magazine, insert a new one, and prepare the weapon.
- Synchronize ammunition count with the reload animation.
- Prevent shooting while reloading.
- Allow reloading to be interrupted when switching weapons.
- Give each weapon its own realistic reload duration and animation.

### 3. TWO-WEAPON INVENTORY SYSTEM
The character can carry ONLY TWO WEAPONS at a time.

- Add two weapon slots.
- Allow the player to equip two different weapons.
- Add a weapon switching button on mobile.
- Allow PC players to switch between weapons using keys 1 and 2.
- Make switching between the two weapons instant or very fast, with smooth animations.
- When switching weapons, place the previous weapon visibly on the character's back.
- Allow the player to drop or replace a weapon to collect another one.
- Never allow the player to carry more than two weapons.

### 4. FIX THE SAFE ZONE SYSTEM
Fix the problem where the Safe Zone does not appear or disappear correctly during combat.

- Make the Safe Zone visible at all times when a match is active.
- Display the Safe Zone boundary clearly on the map and minimap.
- Show the shrinking zone timer.
- Display a warning before the zone starts shrinking.
- Make the zone damage players who stay outside it.
- Update the zone position and size correctly throughout the match.
- Ensure the zone remains visible even when the player is shooting, aiming, fighting, or switching weapons.
- Never hide the zone because of character animations or combat actions.

### 5. FINAL REQUIREMENTS
- Preserve all existing weapons, maps, characters, and controls.
- Ensure all systems work in Battle Royale and Battle Royale Ranked.
- Optimize performance for Android and PC.
- Fix bugs without breaking existing gameplay features.

IMPORTANT: Test every weapon individually and verify that only SCAR, AK, PARAFAL, and AWM have scope functionality.
